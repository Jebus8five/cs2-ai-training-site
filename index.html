CS2-AI Training

Research and development of intelligent training systems and human-like opponents for Counter-Strike 2.

Human Opponent Modelling

The project explores the analysis of Counter-Strike match demonstrations across player skill levels to model realistic human behaviour, decision-making and execution.

The goal is to develop adaptive training opponents that reproduce plausible player behaviour across different skill levels for use in offline and controlled training environments.
